Agent-native product management is the discipline the chapters describe. Maskin is the agentic workspace where that discipline runs: humans keep the judgment, agents do the closing, one shared typed object graph makes team memory compound with every bet.

## The loop, running as product

- **Signal.** Insights land in the workspace as typed objects, from users, agents, and the market. Nothing gets buried in chat or lost between tools.
- **Bet.** A shaped bet with a close condition frames the work. The human gate holds taste on the frame; agents pick up the rest.
- **Shipped outcome.** The loop closes on a validated result. Team memory compounds. The next bet inherits the context, not just the code.

Open-source, MCP-native, self-hostable, BYO model. Apache 2.0.
